About me

Adam Day

I am a relationship counsellor and a registered member of the British Association for Counselling and Psychotherapy (BACP). I work with couples, multiple lovers, families and individuals who want to understand themselves and each other more clearly.

My experience includes long-term therapy, NHS time-limited counselling and prison counselling. Whatever brings you here, my aim is to offer a space where you can speak honestly — real talk — and be heard without judgement.

Services

How I can help

Relationship & couples counselling

For couples and multiple lovers who want to communicate better, work through conflict, rebuild trust or decide what comes next — together.

Individual therapy

One-to-one sessions for anxiety, low mood, relationship patterns, loss or simply feeling stuck — at a pace that suits you.

Family counselling

A neutral space for families to talk through tensions, transitions and changes, and to find new ways of relating to one another.

Group therapy

A weekly online group on Zoom: mixed, 90-minute evening meetings, at least 40 weeks a year, with a maximum of seven participants.

My approach

Integrative, and shaped around you

There is no single method that fits everyone. I work integratively, drawing on different traditions to suit what you need rather than asking you to fit a model.

  • CBT

  • Humanistic
